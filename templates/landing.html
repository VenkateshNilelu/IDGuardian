How it works

One profile, four independent readings

Each layer looks at a completely different slice of the profile — word choice, meaning, behavior, and account telemetry — then a final Trust Fusion model learns how much to weight each one, per platform.

1

Lexical

TF-IDF + Logistic Regression reads bio and caption word choice — fast, and every word's contribution is visible.

2

Semantic

Sentence-BERT embeddings + XGBoost read overall tone and meaning — catches paraphrased fakes lexical alone would miss.

3

Behavioral

XGBoost on 25+ account signals per platform — growth rate, posting rhythm, verification, device diversity.

4

Trust Fusion

Logistic Regression learns, per platform, how much to trust each layer — then outputs one final score.
